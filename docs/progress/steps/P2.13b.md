## Goal
Second half of P2.13. `successor` (Haskell, CouchDB) computes n + 1
through the digit services and `carry` instead of natively: it asks the
digit service of the units digit for its successor (`GET /next`) and,
when that digit reports a carry, calls `carry`
(`aaas.carry.v1.CarryService/Propagate`) for the higher positions. It
serves the gRPC method `Next` of `CLAUDE.md` 4.3
(`contracts/proto/aaas/successor/v1/`) on port 9090, next to the HTTP
form `POST /v1/next` that the ledger keeps until P3.3 (ADR-0020). The
marker `TEMPORARY(P2.13)` is removed. Result, together with P2.13a: real
Peano arithmetic.

## What Was Done
- **`successor` 0.2.0** (`services/successor`):
  - `Successor.Core`: `checkArgument`, `toDigits` and `fromDigits`
    (decimal text, least significant digit first) and `successorVia`: one
    call to the digit service of the units digit and, when it reports a
    carry, one `Propagate(digits, 1)` call; no native `+` on `n`. The
    marker `TEMPORARY(P2.13)` is gone.
  - `Successor.Digits`: HTTP client of `digit-0` … `digit-9`
    (`AAAS_DIGIT_URL_TEMPLATE`), 250 ms per attempt, three attempts with
    jitter, replies checked against the digit contract.
  - `Successor.Carry`: grapesy client of `carry` (`AAAS_CARRY_ADDR`) over
    one HTTP/2 connection, reconnecting every 100–200 ms, a 2 s deadline
    per call and up to three attempts on `UNAVAILABLE`.
  - `Successor.Grpc`: gRPC server of `SuccessorService/Next` on 9090
    (`AAAS_GRPC_PORT`); statuses `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, `OUT_OF_RANGE`,
    `UNAVAILABLE`; exceptions logged as JSON; a drain flag and a counter
    of calls in progress for SIGTERM.
  - `Successor.Rpc`: the method types and the `traceparent` metadata of
    both methods.
  - `Successor.Api.serveCall`: the handling shared by HTTP and gRPC;
    `taskId` must be a lowercase UUIDv7 on both (`isUuidV7`).
  - `Successor.Service.call`: computes every call, retries included,
    through the digits and `carry`, then logs and publishes as before.
  - `Successor.Config`: `AAAS_GRPC_PORT`, `AAAS_DIGIT_URL_TEMPLATE`,
    `AAAS_CARRY_ADDR` (checked at startup).
  - `app/Main.hs`: the digit client, the carry connection, the gRPC
    server next to warp, the drain on SIGTERM.
  - Build: the generated messages of `contracts/gen/haskell` (Successor
    and Carry) as the internal library `successor:proto`, copied from the
    `contracts` build context; grapesy 1.2.1 and its dependencies in
    `cabal.project.freeze` (194 packages); `grpc-spec -snappy`; `protoc`
    in the toolchain stage only.
- **Compose**: `successor` gets the `contracts` build context, port 9090,
  the digit URL template and the carry address, and waits for `carry` and
  the ten digit services to be healthy.
- **Documentation**: README, SERVICE_CHARTER, RACI, SLO, RUNBOOK,
  THREAT_MODEL, DATA_CLASSIFICATION, DEPENDENCIES, CAPACITY, API.md and
  CHANGELOG of `successor`; ADR-0033.

## Decisions
- [ADR-0033](../../adr/0033-successor-through-digits-and-carry-grapesy.md):
  - grapesy 1.2.1 (with http2 5.4.6) for the gRPC server and client;
    `grpc-spec -snappy`; `protoc` in the toolchain stage only;
  - the generated messages as the internal library `successor:proto`;
  - the digits of `n` read and written as decimal text, no native `+`;
  - every call, retries included, recomputed before the stored event is
    replayed (a first version answered retries from the call log; it was
    dropped because it would replay a tampered stored result unchecked);
  - digit calls 250 ms × 3, carry calls with a 2 s deadline and up to
    three attempts on `UNAVAILABLE`, reconnecting every 100–200 ms;
  - `/health` unchanged; `task_id` must be a UUIDv7 on both interfaces;
    `traceparent` as gRPC custom metadata; the shutdown drain.
