## Goal
First half of P2.13, split because the step adds two gRPC servers in two
new runtimes (`carry` in Elixir, `successor` in Haskell). `carry` (Elixir,
Mnesia) serves the gRPC method `Propagate` of `CLAUDE.md` 4.3
(`contracts/proto/aaas/carry/v1/`) on port 9090: it adds one at a decimal
position by asking the digit services for the successor of each visited
digit, continues while they report a carry, records every propagation in
Mnesia and publishes `aaas.carry.propagated.v1`. P2.13b makes `successor`
call it.

## Decisions
- [ADR-0032](../../adr/0032-carry-elixir-mnesia-grpc.md):
  - `grpc_server` on Cowboy, `:httpc` for the digit services, brod under
    its own supervisor;
  - the natural key `{task_id, digits, position}` and the replay of the
    stored event;
  - Mnesia on a non-distributed node, disc-only propagation log, JSON
    migrations, 24 h retention;
  - interactive release mode and one scheduler (measured: 98 → 35 MiB
    at rest, peak 126 → 50 MiB);
  - the two documentation gates and the cowlib advisory.
- The step was split into P2.13a and P2.13b (`CLAUDE.md` 8, rule 11).
